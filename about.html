About StoryVerse

StoryVerse makes good stories easy to find for movie and story lovers. We are a small independent bookstore and movie rental spot made for people who love to read and watch. Started in 2024 in Braamfontein.

To create a welcoming space where every book and story lover can find their next favourite story without breaking the bank.

We are located at 123 Jorissen Street, Braamfontein, Johannesburg. Open Monday to Saturday, 9am to 5pm. Phone: 011 123 4567, Email: info@storyverse.co.za
